# Col du Fantasy, guest view

A one-page handout for an invited guest. Everything here is readable; nothing here asks
them to install, configure or remember anything.

---

## What this is

Col du Fantasy is a friend-group fantasy cycling game, and these are two finished
seasons of it. You are looking at real boards with real results, the same ones the
players used, with the names and addresses taken out.

There are two, and they are different games with different scoring:

- **The Tour board.** A snake draft: eight picks a stage, two per player, nobody can
  take a rider somebody else already has.
- **The Vuelta board.** Fantasy Points, with the side games that grew out of a season of
  arguing about them.

Switch between them from the circular button at the top right.

---

## Signing in

1. Go to the link you were sent.
2. Put in the email address the invitation went to.
3. A six-digit code arrives in a few seconds. Put it in.

That is the whole thing. There is no password to choose and nothing to download. The
code is good for one sign-in and the session lasts, so you will not be asked again on
that device for a long while.

If no code arrives, the address is almost certainly not the one the invitation went to.
Check with Allen rather than trying another address: the system deliberately answers the
same way whether or not an address is on the list, so there is no error to read.

---

## What you will see

A **Guest view** pill under the masthead. That is the only difference between your board
and a player's, and it is there so you always know which one you are on.

Everything that makes the board worth looking at is live for you:

- every stage, its route, its result and how the points fell
- the standings, and the whole season's shape in the charts
- **See Placement**, which switches the whole board between two ways of counting
- the rider cards, the team explorer, the jersey predictions once they were revealed
- the personas, the awards, and the anthem

Tap anything. Charts, tabs, panels and rider cards all work. Changing what you are
looking at is yours to do; it changes nothing for anyone else.

---

## What you cannot do

Make a pick, or change one.

The seats belong to the four players who actually played those seasons. Tapping a rider
opens their story rather than entering them, and the draft card shows the finished picks
rather than a live one. This is not a demonstration restriction that could be lifted for
you: the boards you are on are **copies**, and the real pools are not reachable from your
account at all.

---

## Privacy, in both directions

No real email address exists anywhere on the boards you can see. The copies were built
with every address stripped out and the result re-checked before it was written.

Your own address is held in one place, as a key with a date next to it, so the system
knows when your access ends. It is not on any board and no other guest can see it.

---

## When it ends

Your invitation has an expiry date. On the day after it, the link stops working and the
sign-in stops sending codes. Nothing is deleted and nothing breaks; the board simply
stops opening. If you want longer, ask.

---

## If something looks wrong

Tell Allen what you were looking at and what you expected. A guest board that renders a
blank panel where a finished side game should be is a bug, not a permission, and it is
worth reporting.
